# College Outreach Sequence (3 touches, 2 variants)

Target: Training & Placement (T&P) officers / faculty coordinators at engineering colleges.
Fill the [brackets]. Only state claims you can back up (check each against asyvyas.in before sending).

## Variant A - Value-first (offer a free session)

**Touch 1 (Day 0, email) - Subject: Free live session on [AI/ML | Full Stack | Data Analytics] for [College] students**
Dear [Name] Sir/Madam,
I'm [Your name], a [year] B.Tech student at NIT Agartala, reaching out on behalf of Asyvyas Solutions. We run live online
skill programmes in AI & ML, Full Stack, Data Analytics, Cloud and more.
Would you be open to hosting one free 45-minute introductory session for your [branch] students this month? There is no
cost to the college; students who want to go further can choose a certification.
May I share a short outline? Happy to work around your exam schedule.
Regards, [Name | phone | LinkedIn]

**Touch 2 (Day 3, LinkedIn / WhatsApp)**
Hi [Name], following up on my email about a free skills session for [College] students. Would a 10-minute call this
week work to see if it fits your calendar?

**Touch 3 (Day 7, email) - Subject: Re: Free live session for [College]**
Dear [Name], one last note. I can share the session outline and a sample agenda in one page if useful. If this isn't a
priority right now, no problem, and I'll check back next semester.

## Variant B - Outcome-first (placement relevance)

**Touch 1 (Day 0, email) - Subject: Helping [College] [final/pre-final] year students get interview-ready**
Dear [Name] Sir/Madam,
Many [branch] students tell us they have the degree but lack project work and a certified skill to show in interviews.
Asyvyas Solutions offers live online programmes in [track] that include [verified feature, e.g. live mentorship], priced at
[verified price] per course.
Could I take 10 minutes to show how this could complement your placement preparation?
Regards, [Name | phone | LinkedIn]

**Touch 2 (Day 3, LinkedIn / WhatsApp)**
Hi [Name], sharing a one-page overview of our [track] programme for your students: [link / attachment]. Happy to answer questions.

**Touch 3 (Day 7, email)**
Dear [Name], if a short intro call is possible this week, please tell me a time that suits. If not, I'll reach out after
your upcoming exams.

## Rules for a fair A/B test
- Randomise which colleges get A vs B (alternate down the priority-ranked list).
- Same send time-of-day and channel mix across variants.
- Log every send in the workbook's Outreach_Log sheet the day you send it.
- Need ~30+ sends per variant to say anything; with fewer, report it as directional only.

## Objection handling (for GD/interview practice)
| Objection | Honest response |
|---|---|
| "Our students already have placement training." | "This complements it with project-based, certified skill tracks; we can start with one optional session." |
| "Is it paid?" | "The intro session is free; the certification is optional and priced at [verified price]. Students decide." |
| "How do we know the placement claims are real?" | "Ask for verifiable data (company names, offer counts) and share it with the college; do not repeat claims you cannot show." |
| "Exams are coming." | "Understood - let's pencil a date after exams and I'll follow up then." |
