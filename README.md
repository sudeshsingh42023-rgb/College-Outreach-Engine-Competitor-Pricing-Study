# College Outreach Engine & Competitor Pricing Study

Prospecting + positioning toolkit for a Business Development Associate at an ed-tech / skill-training company
(built around Asyvyas Solutions' public offering: live online certifications, INR 2,499 + GST).

## Contents
| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `asyvyas_outreach_workbook.xlsx` | Competitor benchmark, 100-row college pipeline with tier/size/warm-contact priority scoring, outreach log, A/B summary with z-test, illustrative revenue potential |
| `outreach_templates.md` | 3-touch email/LinkedIn/WhatsApp sequence in two variants (value-first vs outcome-first) plus objection handling |
| `pitch_one_pager.md` | One-page pitch for T&P cell heads (free-pilot offer) |
| `positioning_memo.md` | Positioning memo skeleton (market snapshot, strengths/weaknesses, segments, actions) |
| `ab_test.py` | Two-proportion z-test and Fisher exact test on an outreach log CSV |
| `data/outreach_log_SAMPLE.csv` | **Simulated** 120-send log to demo the script - not real results |

## To finish the project (about 2-3 hours)
1. **Competitor_Benchmark:** fill 8 rows from each company's public site; add source URLs and dates.
2. **College_Pipeline:** list 50-100 real colleges (public T&P pages / AICTE listings), tier them, add contacts.
3. **Send a real batch** (even 30-40 messages, alternating A/B) and log it in Outreach_Log; `AB_Summary` computes reply
   rates and significance.
4. Update the memo with your real numbers, and use only real numbers on your resume.

## Honest-use notes
- Do not quote the sample CSV as results.
- Asyvyas's placement figures are self-reported; the toolkit deliberately leads with demonstrable proof.
